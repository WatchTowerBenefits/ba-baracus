// 01 Who Built This and Why

In the beginning, there was no automation. No workflows, no playbook, no predecessor. Just a blank canvas, a laptop, and a dragon watching from the desk.

Over seven months, one Automation Strategist ventured into the void and returned with production workflows that freed hundreds of hours and saved tens of thousands of gold pieces from the treasury. Carriers were emailed. Tasks were updated. Contacts were routed. And it was good.

What you see here was not handed down from on high. There were ancient tombs -- bloated, crumbling architectures inherited from those who came before, 150 nodes deep and held together by hope. They were opened. They turned to dust. What rose from the rubble was leaner, faster, and built by someone who had to figure out every inch of it alone.

If the Bard's song sounds strange to your ears, seek the tome marked Playlist. All shall be revealed.

The dragon remains. Watching. Judging. Keeping the throughput honest.

// 04 The Philosophy
Every workflow in this suite was built around one principle inherited from a prior life at Groupon: idiot proof design. Not because the people using it are idiots. Because at 2am when something breaks, context is zero and the system needs to be clear enough that anyone can follow it.

That is why there are comments in the code nodes. That is why the node names tell a story. That is why the error handling is graceful instead of loud.

Build things like someone else will have to fix them at 2am. Because they will.
